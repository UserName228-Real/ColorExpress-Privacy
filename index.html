Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Color Express mobile game (the "Application"), operated by E&R Games (the "Service Provider", "we"). It explains what information the Application uses, why, and what choices you have.

Summary

  • The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or payment details.
  • The game is free and supported by advertising. Ads are provided by third-party ad networks, which may collect your device's advertising ID and other device information.
  • When you first open the game we ask whether ad partners may use your advertising ID to personalize ads. You can say no and still play, and you can change your answer at any time.

Information we and our advertising partners collect

When you use the Application, the following information is collected automatically:

  • Your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID)
  • Your device's IP address (which gives only an approximate location, such as country or city)
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version
  • Information about ads shown to you and how you interact with them (for example whether an ad was shown, completed or clicked)

We do not use your precise (GPS) location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, settings (such as sound and language) and your advertising choice are saved only on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Advertising

The Application shows two kinds of ads: ads you can choose to watch to earn extra coins (rewarded ads), and full-screen ads shown between some levels. To show ads, the Application uses Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service, which connects the Application to ad networks, including ironSource and Unity Ads. These partners may use your advertising ID, IP address and device information to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, if you allow it, to personalize ads to your interests. These partners act as independent controllers of the data they collect and have their own privacy policies (linked below).

Your advertising choice

When you first open the Application we ask whether our ad partners may use your advertising ID to show ads that match your interests ("personalized ads").

  • If you choose Allow, ads may be personalized.
  • If you choose Don't allow, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized, and we tell our ad partners that you have declined consent and opted out of the sale or sharing of your data.

You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Purchases

The Application does not offer in-app purchases. Extra coins can only be earned by playing or by watching ads.

How we use information

  • To show ads, which keep the game free
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information for money and we do not send marketing messages.

Third-party services

The Application uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Unity (including Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay)
  • ironSource

Sharing of information

We may disclose information as required by law (for example to respond to a legal request), to protect our rights or the safety of others, or to service providers who work on our behalf and have agreed to follow this policy. Our advertising partners receive the information described above in order to provide ads.

International data transfers

Our ad partners may process data in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Where the law requires it, they use appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. We do not hold an account or profile for you, so most data about you is held by our ad partners; you can contact us and we will help you reach them. You can withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time in Settings > Ad privacy.

California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. You can opt out by choosing "Don't allow" in Settings > Ad privacy, or by contacting us.

Data retention

We do not keep your personal data ourselves. Data collected by our ad partners is kept by them according to their own policies. Game data on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Children

The Application is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take the necessary steps.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, and we limit what the Application collects. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent again.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise your rights, contact E&R Games at colorexpress.support@gmail.com.
